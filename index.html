Password Security

🔓 Weak Passwords

Short and predictable passwords can be easier for attackers to guess.

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords or passphrases that are difficult to guess.

Two-Step Verification (2FA)

Add another layer of protection to your online accounts with two-step verification.

1️⃣ First Step

Enter your username and password to sign in.

2️⃣ Second Step

Confirm your identity using an authenticator app, passkey, or another supported verification method.

🛡️ Better Protection

Even if someone discovers your password, the additional verification step can help protect your account.

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

✔ Use long and unique passwords.

✔ Enable two-step verification.

✔ Never share your passwords.

✔ Avoid suspicious links and emails.

✔ Use a trusted password manager.

✔ Keep your devices and applications updated.
